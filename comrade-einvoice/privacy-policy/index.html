3Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information through Comrade e-Invoice:

  • Account information — name, business/company name, email address, and phone number provided during sign-up.
  • Business and invoicing data — customer/vendor details, invoice line items, amounts, tax details, and payment status that you enter into the App.
  • Device and usage information — device model, operating system version, app version, and basic diagnostic/crash data.
  • Optional permissions — such as storage access (to attach/export invoice files) or camera access (to scan documents/receipts), only if you grant them.

4How We Use Your Data

Information collected through Comrade e-Invoice is used to:

  • Create and manage your account and invoicing records.
  • Generate, store, and let you export or share invoices.
  • Provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App's performance and features.
  • Send important service-related notifications (e.g. updates, invoice reminders).

We do not use your business or invoicing data for advertising.

5Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal or business data. We may share limited information with:

  • Service providers — such as cloud hosting and infrastructure partners who process data on our behalf, solely to operate the App.
  • Legal compliance — where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy continuing to apply to your data.

6Data Security

We apply industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards — including encryption in transit, access controls, and restricted internal access — to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and invoicing data for as long as your account remains active, or as needed to provide the App's services, comply with legal/tax record-keeping obligations, and resolve disputes.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will delete or anonymize your data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.
